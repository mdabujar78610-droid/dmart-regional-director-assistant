# Executive Summary

**Dataset:** DMart synthetic daily store, inventory and feedback data  
**Period:** 1 April–29 June 2026  
**Author:** Mohammad Abuzar

## Headline finding
Pune is the lowest-revenue store, but the premise that it is lowest because it has the most stockouts is unsupported.

## Magnitude
Pune averages INR 636,358.55 revenue per day and 2.32 stockout hours per day. Hyderabad has the highest stockout average at 3.85 hours. Pune records 70 Poor-performance days out of 90.

## Drivers and non-drivers
Cleaned-data revenue-footfall correlation is 0.918, while revenue-stockout correlation is -0.152 across all stores. These are associations, not proof of cause. Pune's stockout duration ranks fourth of five, so highest stockouts do not explain its position.

## Supporting facts
- Pune mean achievement is 70.71%; all-store mean is 91.24%.
- Pune revenue per footfall is INR 419.29.
- Pune-only revenue-footfall correlation is 0.735.

## Open hypotheses and data needed
Unconfirmed: local demand/catchment differences; assortment, pricing or conversion issues. Obtain catchment and competitor data, SKU-level sales and availability, pricing/promotion history, conversion, basket size and customer feedback.

## Recommendation
**Owner:** Pune Store Manager with Regional Merchandising Lead. **Action:** conduct a 14-day SKU availability, pricing and conversion audit and compare results with Mumbai and Bengaluru; **timeline:** deliver a corrective-action plan within 21 days.
